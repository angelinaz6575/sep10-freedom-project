## Content 

So, my solution to my problem is that since there is an network outage that cuts off all networks, I thought of a portable wifi solution where the portable wifi is separated from the main network that's used all around the world, and that it have its own private network. No, not like the mobile hotspot where you can share your wifi with someone by connecting to cellular network. What I mean by portable wifi is that a portable wifi, just like how a portable charger is shaped, but it contains its own wifi that isn't connected to the main network used in everyday life. You'll need to plug your devices into the portable wifi to actually connect to the private network. So when there is another network outage, the portable wifi is the solution to the problem since it can connect the devices to the private networks so you can be back online to do your task. 

## Engineering Design Process

After defining the problem, I came up a solution that might work. Now this is step 3 of the engineering design process which is to brainstorm possible solutions for the problem. My possible solution is a portable network so that business people can get back online with their customers to continue their chatting and other things during a network outage so the communication between workers and the customers is still going, and that the files needed to perform their jobs is saved and not lost due to the network outage.
