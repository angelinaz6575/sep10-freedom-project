So, my solution to my problem is that since there is an network outage that cuts off all networks, I thought of a portable wifi solution where the portable wifi is seperated from the main network that's used all around the world, and that it have its own private network. You'll need to plug your devices into the portable wifi to actually connect to the private network. So when there is another network outage, the portable wifi is the solution to the problem since it can connect the devices to the privatr networks so you can be back online to do your task. 



No, not like the mobile hotspot where you can share your wifi with someone by connecting to cellular network. What I mean by portable wifi is that a portable wifi, just like how a portable charger is shaped, but it contains its own wifi that isn't connected to the main network used in everyday life.
