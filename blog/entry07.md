## Content
We reached the final stage, which was doing our presentations of our Freedom Project. As for the in-class presentation, we had to talk about how we coded our website, came up with this idea, and so on while maintaining a decent amount of eye contact. It was kind of nerve wracking thinking about the presentation, but when I went up in front of the class to actually present, I was calm - I didn't feel scared or nervous and the presentation went well. Maybe because I wasn't overthinking about what I would say during the presentation and how I should be.

Our other presentation before the in-class presentation was our Expo elavator pitch. The entire sep10 people were stationed in the cafeteria and we had to present to the freshmans and juniors/seniors. We watched the sep11 and seniors students present their elevator pitch the day before and it was our turn. Though I must say, it was overwhelming at first because this was my first time with my partner to do this, so this is new to us. But after a few people coming up to us and we presenting to them, I no longer felt overwhelmed.

The next step to test this prototype.
